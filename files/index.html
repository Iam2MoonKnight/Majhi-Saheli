Who We Are

Empowering Communities Across 6 Districts

MSCT Mazi Saheli Charitable Trust works with diverse communities, including high-risk and bridge populations across Thane, Palghar, Ratnagiri, Raigad, Satara, and Osmanabad districts of Maharashtra.

  • Health & Hygiene — 224 camps, 11,962 participants
  • Financial Literacy — 121 sessions, 3,206 participants
  • Skill Building & Education — Learning centers, computer labs, libraries
  • Self-Initiated: Rain water harvesting, kitchen gardens, Eco tourism
