Who We Are

Mazi Saheli Charitable Trust is a grassroots organisation — including high-risk and bridge populations — across 6 districts of Maharashtra: Thane, Palghar, Ratnagiri, Raigad, Satara, and Osmanabad.

We believe that every person, regardless of their social or economic status, deserves access to opportunities and resources for a fulfilling life. Our Mission is to empower rural communities across India by providing essential services in education, healthcare, livelihood, and community development.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision
🌟

A Resilient, Self-Sufficient Rural India

To build a resilient and self-sufficient rural India where every family has access to essential resources, opportunities, and community systems — irrespective of their caste, class, or geography.

Our Mission
🎯

Empowering Communities Across India

To empower rural communities across India by providing essential services in education, healthcare, livelihood, and community development — creating sustainable livelihoods at the family-resource level.

Our Story So Far

2015 – Establishment

MSCT's Beginnings

Mazi Saheli Charitable Trust registered as a formal organisation — a dream shared by founders passionate about rural upliftment in Maharashtra.

2017

Strategic Collaborations

MSCT partnered with 14+ NGOs, State Agencies, Group Homes, and government entities to multiply our reach and impact across Maharashtra.

2019

Reaching Across Districts

Expanded to multiple districts, developing strong chapter chapters with field coordinators and growing community membership to 5,000+ participants.

2020

Learning Centres & Digital Labs

Established dedicated learning facilities: computer labs, digital literacy centres, and vocational training facilities to provide sustainable livelihood skills.

2024 – 21K+ Lives Touched

21,000+ Lives Touched

Spanning across 5 districts and 72 model villages. 12 formal library collections and a strong network of trained community health workers.
